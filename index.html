Office Troll

Team: Sechaba Mokoena, Latita Mvunelo, Sufyaan Albertus, Sandile Duba

Synopsis

You are Mr. Mthembu, a mid-level clerk at the Department of Home Affairs. Every stamp, “missing” file and extra form shapes the fate of hospitals, roads, tourism and ordinary citizens. Balance two forces, the Fat Cats and the Republic, and reach 100 favour with both to master the ultimate bureaucratic balancing act.

Project brief

A university project that turns a real South African community issue into a game: slow, inefficient and sometimes corrupt government services. Players hold the power of an official and see how their choices can serve the public or serve elite interests.

My role and contribution

Level designer and project lead. I led a team of four (designer, artist, programmer and myself) and built the project plan, with its schedule, task tracker, RACI matrix and risk log. I also owned the milestone reports and the final presentation. I also designed the 3D Home Affairs office layout.

Made in Unity 6 for Windows PC. Source code is on GitHub.

Sylvaris: Heartwood Vigil

Tower defence · Team: Sechaba Mokoena, Sufyaan Albertus

Synopsis

Defend the Heartwood against waves of corrupted creatures. Every third wave, a procedurally generated Void Herald arrives, a heavy boss whose traits change how you place towers, prioritise targets and spend Starlight. A Wave Director watches how you play and adapts later waves to your skill.

Project brief

Part 3 of the GADE7322 Portfolio of Evidence. The project explored procedural content generation in a tower defence game, with each team member designing a feature of their own.

My role and contribution

Level designer and project manager. I did the level design, mapping out the terrain, and planned the Void Herald encounter: a five-stage lifecycle (planning, announcement, engagement, resolution and Director feedback), dual-channel trait signalling and counterplay for seven traits. I also set out a testing plan with expressive-range analysis and readability playtests, with a target of 80% correct trait identification.

Technologies used

Project files and build are on GitHub in the Part3 folder.

Rolling Ruins

3D rolling platformer · Team: Sechaba Mokoena, Sufyaan Albertus

Synopsis

Guide Pip, a little roller, through three levels (Beginner, Advanced and Expert). Collect coins and keys, roll over flowers and jump broken bridges, with friendly NPCs offering tips along the way. Patrolling and stationary enemies, projectiles and a boss stand in your way.

Project brief

A platformer prototype built to showcase level progression, with difficulty rising from a gentle tutorial level to an expert challenge.

My role and contribution

Level designer and project manager. I did the level design for all three levels, mapping out the terrain, setting the difficulty of each level and placing the obstacles the player must evade. I also managed the project plan, tracking tasks and keeping the team on schedule.

Technologies used

Unity 6C#Input SystemNavMeshKenney Platformer KitElevenLabs audioClaude (art and UI assistance)

Project files and build are on GitHub. Models from the Kenney Platformer Kit; sound effects and music from ElevenLabs and Kenney Impact Sounds.
